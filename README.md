# Transform Studio

Build a premium, dark-themed demo website for a British luxury fashion brand that designs transformative garments — clothing that physically converts from one silhouette to another (e.g. a shirt that transforms into a dress). This is a high-end ecommerce concept site, so the design should feel artistic, sophisticated, and "quiet luxury" — minimal, spacious, confident — never busy or template-like.

AESTHETIC DIRECTION

- Dark theme throughout: deep charcoal/near-black backgrounds (#0A0A0A – #121212), not pure black

- Accent palette: one refined metallic or muted tone (champagne gold, bone white, or soft taupe) used sparingly for CTAs and highlights — no bright colours

- Typography: an elegant high-contrast serif for headings (editorial, fashion-magazine feel) paired with a clean minimal sans-serif for body text and UI

- Generous whitespace (dark-space), large imagery, slow confident pacing — nothing cramped

- Subtle motion only: gentle fade/parallax on scroll, soft cross-fades between images — nothing gimmicky

STRUCTURE

1. Full-screen hero — looping video/image placeholder of a garment mid-transformation, minimal headline (e.g. "One Garment. Infinite Form.") and a single understated CTA

2. Brand story section — short editorial copy on the transformation concept, British craftsmanship, sustainability-through-versatility angle

3. Featured "Transform" showcase — a before/after or scrub-through visual module showing a piece shifting from shirt to dress (use a placeholder image swap/slider interaction to demonstrate the concept)

4. Shop/Collection grid — product cards with large imagery, hover reveals a second angle, price, "shirt ↔ dress" tag; include at least 6 placeholder products

5. Individual product page template — large gallery with zoom, multiple views, a short transformation video placeholder, size/fit info, "Add to Bag", trust details (shipping, returns)

6. Email/waitlist signup section — for launch list capture, minimal single-field form

7. Footer — social links (Instagram), legal pages placeholders (privacy, shipping, returns), newsletter signup

UX REQUIREMENTS

- Fully responsive, mobile-first — this audience will browse heavily on mobile

- Sticky minimal navbar (logo centered or left, Shop / Story / Contact), transparent over hero, solid dark on scroll

- Fast-feeling, uncluttered navigation — max 4-5 nav items

- Cart drawer (slide-in) rather than a separate page

- Checkout and account pages can be simple placeholders — the focus is the aesthetic and shopping experience, not backend logic

TONE FOR ALL PLACEHOLDER COPY

Confident, minimal, editorial — short sentences, no exclamation marks, no generic ecommerce filler ("Shop Now!", "Amazing deals"). Think luxury fashion house voice, not high-street retail.

Use placeholder product images/videos that suggest movement and fabric, and generate 6-8 sample products (name, price in GBP, short one-line description) themed around convertible/transformable garments.
